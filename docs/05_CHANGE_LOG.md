## Behaviour changes (all documented in the audit; none silently alter a formula)

1. KPI cards, the map, the division and institute tables, analysis and metric pages and the department list now use **exact** aggregates for every filter combination. 1.0 approximated some combinations (audit A5).
2. Drill-through and equipment lists are **complete**. 1.0 truncated them to 12 items (audit A6).
3. The Equipment Explorer applies **all** filters and is paginated. 1.0 used the top 60 of the state-wide list.
4. Equipment names that differ only in letter case are merged (configurable, audit A7).
5. `functional_shortfall` is now populated (audit A3, pending approval).
6. The chatbot answers equipment × location exactly. 1.0 printed a state-wide disclaimer.
